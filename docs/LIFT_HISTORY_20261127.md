## Native lifted DC
The high-rate DC blocker H(z)=(1-z^-1)/(1-p z^-1) can be lifted through decimation by M using 1/(1-p z^-1)=(sum_{j=0}^{M-1}p^j z^-j)/(1-p^M z^-M). Convolve the decimation filter with that finite geometric numerator and apply one base-rate recurrence with p^M. Mode 1 includes the difference in the convolved taps; mode 2 differences the shaped samples before convolution, preserving exact constant-input zero at the differencer. Mode 3 instead keeps the high-rate DC blocker and pairs symmetric interpolation taps in phase zero and half-factor. Same 40-sample alignment and unchanged dry path. Fixed prepare settings, not live parameter automation. Mathematical identity ignores denormal flush differences and finite arithmetic.
