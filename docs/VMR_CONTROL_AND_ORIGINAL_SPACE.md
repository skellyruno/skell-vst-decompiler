# VMR control stream evidence and original spatial DSP — 2026-11-24

## Static binary research

`python -m skell_vst_decompiler.vmr_control /private/path/FG-116.epl` (also FG-2A.epl or FG-Stress.epl) validates the exact SHA-256, local instruction operands, numeric constants, and the seven explicit FG-2A vector-dispatch slots. The combined report `reports/vmr_control_streams.json` is indexed by the existing research catalog. All 92 checks passed on the supplied original binaries. These are local relationships in candidate code paths; not full vendor algorithms or runtime validation.

- **FG-116:** at `0x1619a0`, scalar gain slews toward its target with per-iteration coefficient 0.0025 (state initialization can set the target directly). At `0x161880`, a separate buffer helper computes `s=min(x,x+a*(s_prev-x))` and stores state and output. The caller at `0x161d42` passes a coefficient from object field `0x10`. For a retention between zero and one the helper captures decreases immediately, smooths increases. The input stream semantics, sample-rate domain and complete gain-target law remain open.
- **FG-2A:** at `0x155960`, two per-channel scalar ramps use the same 0.0025 coefficient before adding their weighted streams. The call to `0x6f860` reaches a seven-slot vector table at `0x1015f58`; scalar/SSE and AVX addition paths were checked. This resolves the helper's purpose, not the identities of the two weighted audio streams. No assertion of dry/wet mixing, compression ratios or optical circuit parity.
- **FG-Stress:** at `0x163442`, following the previously mapped linked peak state, two bounded streams satisfy `U=clamp(130*(P-T1),0,1)` and `V=(1-U)*clamp(130*(P-T0),0,1)` for finite inputs. Their scratch output is subsequently passed to summary calls and 1024-position circular histories. That evidence does not establish that either stream is the actual applied gain. They may carry monitoring/control data; effect on audio unverified.

The reports identify source builds without distributing their binaries or calibration arrays. No licensing or protection routines analyzed or changed.

## Original Skelliere spatial prototype

`experiments/skelliere/OriginalSpace.h` independently implements an eight-lane **normalized Hadamard** FDN, two allpass diffusion cells per input channel, fixed individually selected delays, per-lane decay gains and one-pole damping, plus a linked rational wet-output duck envelope. It intentionally does not use the previously studied vendor's Householder matrix, delays, coefficients or calibration. Configuration allocates all delay buffers; `wet()` does not allocate or lock. No JUCE or plugin state changes. Zero added processing lookahead, with natural wet-path delay onset. No real-time host or parameter-automation claim.

Compiled C++ tests passed at 44.1, 48, 96 and 192 kHz (finite output, tail decay, duck bound, reset, non-finite inputs) plus address/undefined-behavior sanitizers. An optimized standalone benchmark including sine-input generation on AMD EPYC 9V74 measured about 58 ns/stereo frame in one local run (not DAW CPU, not a sustained real-time deadline test). The independent render harness ran on a private 12-second music excerpt from the user's recording and synthetic impulse responses at 44.1/48/96 kHz. The aggregate report contains no source audio. At 48 kHz, the T20-derived RT60 estimates are 1.816 s (250 Hz), 1.529 s (1 kHz), 0.723 s (4 kHz), 0.383 s (8 kHz). Low-frequency decay is consistent across tested rates; high-frequency decay is sample-rate dependent (0.366–0.383 s at 8 kHz). Wet level reduction on active music was 7.42 dB. Tail in the first silent second remains 6.51 dB down relative to the unducked render, so recovery deserves further tuning before production. RMS-matched listening files are private. No subjective quality or commercial parity claim.
