## Bass-aware recovery
New BassAwarePeakGuard retains the quiet-window polarity bounds and fixed fastest pole r used to precompute exponential release deficits. On each delayed output input sample, compute stereo-linked 120Hz one-pole lowpass and full/low energy with 20ms smoothing, map fraction .15-.80 to a blend, interpolate actual recovery pole from r to max(r,pole(60ms)). Input to detector clamped +/-32 for bounded energy. Because actual recovery pole R>=r, and 0<=g<=1, R*g+1-R <= r*g+1-r; existing release-deficit bound remains valid even with changing R. The bound may be conservative. No pitch detector or universal true-peak guarantee. Latency 16 samples. Six-rate stress 1,734,912 channel/phase checks per optimized and sanitized run; max declared-kernel .891250938133746. Controlled tones 16 conditions x3 (20/30/50/80/100/200/1000/4000Hz, peak1.5/3, 48k), interior last coherent second, 48 renders. 12/16 THD improved >.1dB vs fixed1ms, none worsened >.1dB.
